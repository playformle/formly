How to Play

  1. All hidden words relate to the theme shown above the grid.
  2. Click connected letters (touching in any direction) to trace a word.
  3. Hit Submit Word when you think you've found one.
  4. Found words light up in colour and appear below the grid.
  5. Find the Spangram — one special word that sums up the whole theme.
  6. Find every word to complete the puzzle!
